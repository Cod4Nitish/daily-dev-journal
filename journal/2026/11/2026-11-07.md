# Day 4 · 2026-11-07 · RAG chunking strategies

_Category: AI/RAG · Source: gemini_

Today I learned that RAG performance isn't just about the model, but how you slice your data. I spent the morning experimenting with chunking strategies and realized that the "fixed-size" approach is a great baseline, but it lacks nuance. By splitting text into 512-token chunks with a 10% overlap, I maintained enough context between segments to prevent losing critical information at the boundaries.

However, I also learned that structure matters more than character count. For technical documentation, splitting by Markdown headers or paragraphs keeps semantic units intact, preventing the embedding model from getting confused by fragmented thoughts. It is a constant trade-off: smaller chunks improve precision by matching specific queries, but larger chunks are necessary for capturing broader, thematic context. I’ve started implementing a hybrid approach using LangChain’s `RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter` to balance these needs.

```python
text_splitter = RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter(
    chunk_size=500,
    chunk_overlap=50,
    separators=["\n\n", "\n", " ", ""]
)
```

Moving forward, I will prioritize structural boundaries over arbitrary sizes.
